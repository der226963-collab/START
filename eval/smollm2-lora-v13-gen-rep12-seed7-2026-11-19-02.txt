Phosphorus is a limit that cannot be wished away, because the rivers carry whatever the fields cannot hold. It is an absolute that no one can escape. We have a strong incentive to avoid it, and we do so in many ways: by reducing the amount of phosphorus applied per unit area or growing less-fertilized crops; by recycling what waste water there is through wetlands, lakes, rivers and oceans; and even by storing excess nutrients on land as organic matter in soils rather than in air or water.
