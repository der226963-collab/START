The soil in the lower field is tired after years of the same crop, and the farmers are talking about what to plant instead.
They remember the drought of 1976-8, when irrigation was inadequate and many farmers could not grow enough food. They also recall heavy rains in late spring that wiped out some crops but allowed others to be planted early. This year they say bad weather has been limited by good planning, which includes diversifying into vegetables or fruit instead of just a few grains
