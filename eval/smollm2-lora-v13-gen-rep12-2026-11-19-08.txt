The river runs low in August, and the farms, the town, and the fish all need water from the same shrinking stream. At last the government decides that it must buy out the farmers, and builds a dam at Dungeness to hold back Lake George. The people of Old Town are not happy about this, but they have nowhere else to go—and so Duggan's river is bought by Mr Pitt, who makes his own money buying up other people's land too."
