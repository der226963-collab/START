The community garden needs a plan for the spring, and the first question is how much land each household will tend. We estimate that for every person, three or four square metres of garden space is needed. So we work out how many people live in a house: me plus my partner and kids; then divide by the number of small spaces per head (at least 1m2) to get figures for everybody's share. For example, two persons living in six tiny plots each would need five m² apiece—a fair allocation even if you don't own any land yourself!
