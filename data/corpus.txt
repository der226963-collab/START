The garden grows food for the street. People share the work and share the harvest.
A plan starts with a goal. A goal needs a first step. A first step needs a person and a time.
The community kitchen cooks on Sundays. Neighbours bring bread and soup and stories.
We keep the lights on with sun and wind. We store what we do not use today for tomorrow.
A good meeting ends with a decision. A decision names who does what and by when.
The library lends books and tools. A tool shared is a tool used well.
We plant in spring and water in summer. We save seeds in autumn for the next year.
Care is work. Work shared fairly is lighter for everyone.
